Dear Chair De Ferranti, Vice Chair Coffey, Ms. Cunningham, Mr. Karantonis, Mr. Spain, and County Manager Schwartz, I am an Arlington resident writing to ask the Board to reconsider the proposed 44% fee increase for competitive gymnastics advanced at Thursday's budget markup. The AAPA and ATPA parent associations have proposed a 25% stepped fee increase that reaches the 86% cost recovery target over a reasonable timeline. The proposed 44% increase would place Arlington's fees between $775 and $7,634 above local private gyms depending on level, making our public program the more expensive option for families. Before Wednesday's vote, I respectfully ask the Board to adopt the 25% stepped approach and pair any fee action with a directive that holds DPR accountable for the structural issues (enrollment growth, staffing, overhead methodology) rather than transferring the full consequence to current and future Aerials and Tigers families.
